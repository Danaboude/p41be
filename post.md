History repeats itself, prompting us to reflect on our learnings. As we consider task automation, particularly with the speed rise of hashtag#AI, it is essential to identify the optimal tasks for automation while evaluating the impact on individuals involved. The emergence of new hashtag#AIautomation solutions necessitates a thorough assessment of the hashtag#socialsystem.

An insightful video explores the analysis conducted by Eric Trist and Ken Bamford in 1949 at the Tavistock Institute. Their study of miners' work led to the development of socio-technical systems. However, we must critically examine whether teamwork, as described by Trist in his seven principles, is truly feasible. The question of how far we can progress in "self-management" remains pertinent. 

Determining what to automate and what to retain poses a significant challenge in creating a smooth and efficient organization.
